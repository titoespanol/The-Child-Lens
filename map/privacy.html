Directory and professional accounts

This is a professional ecosystem directory, not a service for children to create accounts or submit health records. Do not provide patient information, confidential research or sensitive personal information.

We curate publicly available professional organisation and research information for ecosystem discovery, relying on legitimate interests. Public availability does not remove data protection rights. You may request a correction, removal or object to processing.

You can browse without an account. If you register, we use your email and account identifier to authenticate you and operate your account. Account sign-in uses email links and Google when Google sign-in is configured; LinkedIn sign-in is not offered. We request information when you sign in, not inbox, contacts or employment-history access. Supabase Auth stores authentication records and may retain provider metadata and tokens. Provider sign-in is subject to that provider’s policies.

We use account and directly entered profile information to provide the service you request, relying on contractual necessity where applicable. A professional profile is optional: you can sign in just to manage email preferences, without providing a professional name, bio or affiliation. Your professional name, role, bio, interests, chosen account picture and self-reported organisation affiliations are currently saved privately, not published as a public person listing. Organisation affiliation does not verify representation. Your email is not published.

Organisation suggestions

Only signed-in members can submit in-app organisation suggestions. Suggestions are associated with the submitting account, checked for required fields and subject to submission limits. A city and country help reviewers place the organisation; they do not create a map pin automatically. An optional address must be the organisation’s public business address, never a home or patient address. It remains in the private queue until review and may be used for the organisation record after approval. They remain private until human review. You can also email map@thechildlens.com with public evidence links. Email suggestions include your sender address and any details you choose to provide. Suggestions stay in a private review queue until a human checks and approves information for the directory. Do not send home addresses, private third-party contacts, patient details or confidential material. Sending a suggestion does not subscribe you to emails. Older submissions may contain an optional contact email supplied under an earlier form; those details remain private and can be the subject of a deletion request.

Organisation logos

People with a saved organisation affiliation, approved organisation members and moderators may upload, replace or remove an organisation logo. Logos are visible when the organisation’s core details are public; upload only branding you are authorised to use, never confidential or personal information. Images are resized, converted to PNG and stripped of original metadata. Saved affiliations are self-reported and grant logo-management access for that organisation; this does not confer broader organisation editing or sharing permissions.

Optional profile picture

You can choose an existing connected Google or LinkedIn identity picture, upload your own picture, or use no picture. Provider images contact the provider’s image service; LinkedIn sign-in is no longer offered. Uploaded JPEG, PNG or WebP images are cropped to a square and converted to JPEG on your device before upload, removing the original image metadata. The upload is held in a private Supabase Storage bucket under your account and downloaded through your authenticated account and displayed locally in your browser. It is used only in your private professional profile and preview, not public affiliation cards, marketing or contact records. Choosing a provider picture or no picture removes the custom-photo choice and requests deletion of the uploaded file. Account deletion requests must also remove uploaded images through the storage service. Upload only a picture you are entitled to use.

Optional public affiliations

Your professional profile stays private. You can separately choose to make each saved organisation affiliation public. This submits a snapshot of your professional name, role and organisation for human review. After approval, those fields appear in the organisation’s public affiliation list labelled self-reported; it is not verified employment or endorsement. Your email, date of birth, photo, bio and contact choices are excluded. You may withdraw an affiliation immediately by turning its public choice off. Removing the affiliation from your saved private profile also withdraws it. Editing your name or role does not silently replace an already reviewed public snapshot; submit the visibility choice again to request review of updated details. Review decisions are recorded privately for audit. Publication is optional and does not affect account access or email choices.

Optional contact preferences

No professional profile is needed to keep up to date. Sign-in verifies account access and lets you manage choices. Registration and profile saving do not subscribe you to promotional emails. Enter your contact email independently; it is not prefilled from a sign-in provider. For now it must match your verified sign-in email. A different contact address requires separate verification, which is not implemented.

We rely on your consent for two separate, optional purposes:

  • “The Child Lens may email me about opportunities in children's health.” This covers The Child Lens updates and opportunities, including jobs, funding, events, venture-building support and partnerships.
  • “The Child Lens may email me opportunities from its partners and sponsors in children's health.” The Child Lens sends these messages; sponsors and partners do not receive your contact details under this choice. Sponsored content and the relevant organisation must be identified in the message.

Both boxes start unticked for new choices. Earlier consent is not automatically transferred to the revised purposes. When you explicitly save these choices, they replace earlier email preferences. We record the directly entered address, authenticated account identifier, purposes, notice version and server time, separately from professional profile details. Provider-derived names, pictures and other profile content are not copied into marketing records or used for promotional targeting. Consent does not override provider contractual restrictions.

Manage or withdraw your choices here, or email privacy@thechildlens.com. Untick either purpose and save to withdraw that purpose; unticking both and saving clears the active optional contact address. Withdrawal does not affect your account, profile or the lawfulness of earlier processing. A future specific introduction that discloses private details requires separate permission. No automated marketing, CRM export, partner contact-sharing or individual open/click tracking is implemented. Authentication email delivery is separate from these choices.

Suppliers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, database storage and authentication email delivery, Vercel for hosting, Google sign-in and Google or LinkedIn profile-picture services for identities already connected to an account, and external map and code-delivery services including CARTO, jsDelivr and unpkg. MapLibre is the map software. Requests to external services can disclose IP address, browser and request information. Service providers may process information outside the EEA; the applicable transfer mechanism depends on the provider, recipient and configuration. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data. We do not sell provider data or private contact records.

Account age eligibility

Accounts are available to people aged 16 and over. After sign-in, onboarding asks for your date of birth once. It is sent securely to check eligibility and cleared from the form. The account stores only the confirmation time and minimum age, not the birth date, so you are not asked again on later sign-ins or other devices. This is a self-declared check, not identity or age verification. Contact privacy@thechildlens.com if you believe an account belongs to someone under 16.

Saved organisations and sign-in

You can save organisations privately to your signed-in account and remove them there. A saved-list URL opens only the signed-in person’s own list; it does not share account data. Earlier browser-only saves are not automatically assigned to an account. Unsaved onboarding drafts stay only in the current tab session. Sign in using Google or an email sign-in link; the app does not request a password.

Storage and retention

See our Cookies and browser storage notice for sign-in storage, unfinished drafts and browser controls.

Authentication uses browser storage to keep your session. We do not deploy advertising trackers or analytics cookies in this release. Suppliers may process technical logs for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

We retain account and profile records while the account operates, subject to deletion requests and necessary legal obligations. Withdrawal clears the active optional contact address when neither purpose remains selected. Preference history may remain as evidence of choices and withdrawals; account deletion currently cascades to those profile and preference records. We review continued need for retained data and handle deletion requests through the contact above. Public records are reviewed for relevance and accuracy. Removing public directory information does not necessarily remove legally required security or administrative records.

Your rights and requests

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, object to legitimate-interest processing and withdraw consent. Email privacy@thechildlens.com to request account deletion, provider disconnection or a correction. These requests are handled by The Child Lens; signing out alone does not delete your account or revoke the provider’s authorisation. We may request proportionate identity verification, with alternatives if you have no organisation-domain email. You can complain to Spain’s AEPD.

Research and changes

AI tools may help find and structure public professional information. Listings should not be treated as verification of a clinical claim, person or affiliation. We update this notice when practices change and seek new consent where required before additional uses.
