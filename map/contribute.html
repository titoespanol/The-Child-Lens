Community contribution guidelines

We welcome public, professional evidence about child-first startups, pre-spinout research, hospitals, funders and ecosystem enablers. Adult-first products with incidental paediatric uses are not automatically eligible.

Submit and review

Only signed-in members can submit in-app organisation suggestions. Sign in through My account to suggest an organisation for human review. Submission limits apply. You can also email map@thechildlens.com with public evidence links. Do not submit patient information, confidential research or private third-party contacts. Suggestions are reviewed against inclusion criteria and may be edited or declined. AI may assist research and structuring; it does not establish accuracy. Contact map@thechildlens.com to ask about a submission or request review. No fixed turnaround is promised.

Corrections and removal

Email privacy@thechildlens.com for data corrections or removal requests. We may ask for proportionate verification, with alternatives to an organisation-domain email. See our Privacy Notice and Terms of Use.
