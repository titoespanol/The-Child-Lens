About Mapping Children’s Health

Mapping Children’s Health is an evolving public directory by The Child Lens. It helps people explore organisations, research and connections advancing children’s health innovation.

What does the map cover?

The directory includes hospitals, startups, research organisations and other participants in the children’s health ecosystem. Use the directory filters to explore organisation types, countries, specialties and technologies.

How accurate are map locations?

Locations may be approximate. A marker can represent an organisation’s city or headquarters rather than a street address or a place providing care. Some organisations share a location, and records without supported coordinates appear only in the directory. Check the organisation’s own website before visiting or relying on a location.

How should I use a listing?

Listings provide a starting point for discovery. Inclusion is not an endorsement, a clinical recommendation, or a guarantee that information is complete or current. Check an organisation’s own website and the listed sources before relying on its information.

How can I suggest an organisation or correction?

Signed-in members can suggest an organisation using Suggest on the map. Suggestions are reviewed before publication. For corrections, contact map@thechildlens.com and include the organisation name, the change and a supporting public source.

How is AI used?

We currently use AI tools to help research and structure public directory information. People review suggestions before publication. AI-assisted information may contain errors; check the listed sources and tell us about corrections. We review changes to how we use personal information and update our privacy notice where required.

Is my account information public?

Account information and saved organisations are private to your signed-in account. An affiliation appears publicly only when you choose to share it and it is approved for publication.
