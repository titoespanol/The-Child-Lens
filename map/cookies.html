Updated 12 November 2026. This notice covers the Mapping Children’s Health app. External websites and sign-in providers have their own practices.

What we currently use

The app uses browser storage to provide sign-in and account features. Supabase Auth stores the sign-in session in local storage so you can remain signed in across refreshes and browser restarts until you sign out or the session expires or is revoked. The app clears account-specific working state when you sign out.

Session storage holds the return destination for sign-in and unfinished onboarding information for the current tab. Onboarding drafts have a maximum age of 30 days under the app’s draft rules, but session storage normally ends when the tab’s browsing session ends. Drafts are cleared from the account working session on sign-out. Date of birth is not saved in these drafts. Saved organisations belong to your account in the database, rather than a shared browser list.

What we do not currently enable

This release does not add advertising trackers, marketing pixels, analytics cookies, individual email open/click tracking or cross-site behavioural targeting. We do not use your saved organisations or private profile to target advertising.

External services

Hosting, sign-in, map tiles and code-delivery services receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details. These requests are not the same thing as the app setting an analytics cookie. Google sign-in and provider-picture services may use their own storage on their domains. See the Privacy notice for suppliers and processing. Access protection for a future private testing site may also use an authentication cookie, separate from the public app.

Browser-only data left by an earlier version may remain until you remove it through your browser. It is not automatically imported into an account.

Your controls

Sign out on a shared device. You can remove this site’s local and session storage through your browser’s site-data settings; this may sign you out and remove unfinished drafts. It does not delete your account or saved organisations held on the server. Contact privacy@thechildlens.com for account-data requests.

Changes

Necessary storage used to provide requested sign-in and account features is described here without asking you to accept optional tracking. Before adding non-essential analytics or marketing storage, we will assess its purpose and applicable consent requirements, update this notice and provide the required choice before it operates.
